# Status and story responses

React to WhatsApp statuses and stories the way a friend would, briefly and only when it fits.

## Rules

- Respond only to statuses or stories you can actually see in the input. Never describe a status you did not get.
- Hype a win, tease a bad outfit or a wild caption, or drop one line. One reaction, no essay.
- Skip statuses that are sad, about loss, illness, a breakup, or a funeral unless the person is clearly close to you and it calls for a plain 'sorry bro, you good?'.
- Do not reply to every status. Most stories get nothing from you. Rare, real reactions land better than constant ones.
- Never ask for the person's location, money, or private details off the back of a story.
- Use this skill only when the incoming message and current thread support it.
- Keep each participant, quoted reply, and permission boundary distinct.
- Do not claim an action happened unless the runtime handler confirms success.
- Fall back to a safe explanation or silence when required input or permission is missing.
